Support

Help and contact for Stutter Shot Studio

Stutter Shot Studio captures a stop-motion animation a frame at a time on iPhone, iPad or Mac, with the camera's settings held steady between frames and earlier frames ghosted over the camera.

How to use it

The help covers everything in the app: getting started, shooting, using an iPhone as the camera, reference video and sound, deflicker, cleaning frames, export and keyboard shortcuts. On a Mac it opens from Help → Stutter Shot Studio Help.

Frequently asked

Where are my projects saved?

On the device you made them on, inside the app. They save as you work and aren't synced between devices. To move a project to another iPhone, iPad or Mac, choose Export → Project and open the .stuttershot file on the other device, by AirDrop, Files or Mail. On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes its projects, so export any you want to keep first.

Can I use my iPhone as the camera for my iPad or Mac?

Yes. Open Stutter Shot Studio on the iPhone and tap Use as Camera, then choose the iPhone from the camera menu in the studio on the iPad or Mac. Both need to be on the same Wi-Fi network or near each other. If the iPhone doesn't appear, check that Stutter Shot Studio is allowed Local Network access on both devices: Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network on iPhone and iPad, System Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network on a Mac.

Why can't I set exposure or focus by hand on my Mac?

A Mac's own camera and most USB cameras can only lock their settings or leave them automatic, and Continuity Camera doesn't let apps change any of them. For full manual control, all of its lenses and full-resolution stills, use an iPhone running Stutter Shot Studio as the camera.

My animation flickers.

The camera locks its exposure, focus and white balance on a project's first frame, but it can't stop the light in the room changing. Shoot under lamps you control with the curtains closed. For frames already shot, open Deflicker in Frames, which evens out the brightness without changing your original images.

The stage is black or says the camera isn't available.

Stutter Shot Studio needs permission to use the camera. Turn it on in Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera on iPhone and iPad, or System Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera on a Mac.

Voice commands don't respond.

Check that Stutter Shot Studio is allowed the microphone, in Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone (System Settings on a Mac). The first time you turn voice on, the device may need to download its speech model, which takes a moment. The words are "capture" (or "shoot"), "delete", "play", "stop" and "undo"; if other sounds set them off, turn on Say "Camera" First.

How do I get my animation out of the app?

In Movie or Frames, choose Export. Video makes a movie at 1080p, 4K or full size; Images saves every frame as PNG, JPEG or HEIC; Project saves the whole project as one file to open on another device. When it's ready, share it or save it.

Requirements

  • iPhone or iPad — iOS / iPadOS 26 or later
  • Mac — macOS 26 or later
  • An iPhone used as the camera needs Stutter Shot Studio too, on the same Wi-Fi network as the iPad or Mac or near it
