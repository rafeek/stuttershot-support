Privacy policy

Last updated 14 October 2026

Stutter Shot Studio does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no advertising, no analytics services, and no third-party SDKs. Nothing you make is sent to me or to any server.

What the app stores

  • Your projects — the frames you capture, the images and videos you import, reference video and sound, and each project's settings — are stored on the device you made them on, inside the app. They aren't uploaded or synced anywhere.
  • Your settings are stored locally on the device.
  • Exports go only where you send them: a movie, images or a project file you share or save.

On iPhone and iPad, deleting the app deletes its projects and settings.

Camera and microphone

  • The camera captures the frames of your animation and records reference video when you ask it to.
  • The microphone is used only while you record a reference with sound, or while voice commands are on.
  • Voice commands are recognised on the device by Apple's speech recognition. What the microphone hears isn't recorded or saved. The first time, the device may download its speech model from Apple.
  • The words in a reference's sound are transcribed on the device, so that a word said by the reference isn't taken as a command, and the transcript is kept with the project.
  • Finding hands and people in shot, checking the set, deflicker and cleaning frames all analyse your images on the device.

Photos

When you import from Photos, the app receives only the photos and videos you pick. It saves to your photo library only when you choose to save an export there.

Local network

To use an iPhone as the camera for an iPad or Mac, Stutter Shot Studio finds the iPhone on your local network or nearby, and the two connect directly. The camera picture, captured frames and recordings pass only between your two devices, never through a server.

What the app does not do

  • No personal information is collected — no name, email, contacts, or location.
  • No identifiers are used for tracking, and no data is shared with data brokers or advertisers.
  • No usage analytics are collected or uploaded.
  • The app contacts no servers of its own. Apart from the speech model Apple may download, it works without an internet connection.

Children

Stutter Shot Studio is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If a future version of Stutter Shot Studio collects anything, I will update this policy before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
